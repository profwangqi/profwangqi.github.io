研究领域聚焦：AI Agent（智能体）与中国经济结构转型。 研究人工智能等科技创新对中国经济结构转型的影响。具体微观层面而言：基于多源微观大数据，使用简约估计和结构估计的实证方法，研究Agent智能体对公司金融行为和家庭金融行为的影响。

Research Focus: AI Agents and China's Structural Transformation. At the micro level, drawing on multi-source micro-level big data and empirical methods including reduced-form and structural estimation, my research examines the impact of AI agents on corporate financial behavior and household financial behavior.
